Three crates, all load-bearing — and what we sent back upstream

fold — four KeyedStreams (document · library · lenses · reader-cache); scores, percentile histograms, and LLM gists as incrementally-maintained views; algebraic retraction is the corrigendum; Meter operators narrate every edit from inside the dataflow.
↑ upstream: the Meter operator · Bm25 same-tx retract+insert fix · Hnsw sink last-write-wins fix
ese — static embeddings compiled into the binary; a pure function of the text, which is exactly fold's retraction contract; sentence-anchored axes; 3.4µs means the lens never lags the pen.
finding: word anchors are mush — anchor axes with sentences
anny — HNSW nearest-voices over every shelf; true deletion (retract a sentence, its cards release — no tombstones); graph snapshots adopted from Séance (PR #6) and hardened with a rows-content digest.
