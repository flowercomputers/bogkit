the anchored axis · benched at startup, under a millisecond
How many examples make an axis? We measured.

The concrete——abstract axis is a centroid difference over example sentences. Nested anchor sets scored against a held-out 30-sentence ladder in six graded bands — topics overlap across bands, so the axis can't cheat by scoring topic. — cross-band pairs.

Ordering gains taper in the tens; the dynamic range doubles from 4 to 20. Today's instrument stays at 4/pole — changing anchors re-scores the world, and a materialized view keeps its history honest. The v2 axis ships 20+ with this bench as its receipt.

fold · ese · anny
Three crates, all load-bearing — and what we sent back.
fold
Five keyed streams: document, library, lenses, lens definitions, reader cache. Scores, percentile histograms, and LLM gists as incrementally-maintained views; algebraic retraction is the corrigendum; Meters narrate every edit from inside.
↑ upstream: the Meter operator · Bm25 same-tx retract+insert fix · Hnsw sink last-write-wins fix
ese
Static embeddings compiled into the binary — a pure function of the text, which is exactly fold's retraction contract. Sentence-anchored axes; 3.4µs means the lens never lags the pen.
finding: word anchors are mush — anchor axes with sentences
anny
HNSW nearest-voices over every shelf; true deletion — retract a sentence and its cards release, no tombstones. Graph snapshots adopted from Séance (PR #6), hardened with a rows-content digest.
